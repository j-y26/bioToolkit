# Reversed magma without its ends

Light orange (low) to near-black purple (high): magma reversed and
trimmed (5 still visible on a white or transparent background and the
highest are not black. The default of
[`plot_rank()`](https://j-y26.github.io/bioToolkit/reference/plot_rank.md)
(numeric colours) and of a single panel of
[`plot_enrichment_dots()`](https://j-y26.github.io/bioToolkit/reference/plot_enrichment_dots.md).

## Usage

``` r
magma_r_trimmed()
```

## Value

256 colours, from low to high
